(As of March 2026, the state of Uttarakhand conducts government job recruitments through three primary commissions and boards. Each body is responsible for different "Groups" of services based on pay scale and responsibility.

1. Uttarakhand Public Service Commission (UKPSC)
The UKPSC is the premier body for Group A and B (Gazetted) positions.

Combined State Civil/Upper Subordinate Services (PCS): The most prestigious exam for roles like SDM, Deputy SP, and Treasury Officer. (2026 Prelims: May 17; Mains: July 5).

Combined State Lower Subordinate Services (Lower PCS): For posts like Nayab Tehsildar and Supply Inspector.

Judicial Service Civil Judge Exam: Recruitment for the state's junior judiciary. (2026 Prelims: April 21-24).

Review Officer (RO) & Assistant Review Officer (ARO): Conducted for the Uttarakhand Secretariat, UKPSC, and the Advocate General’s Office.

Assistant Engineer (AE): Technical recruitment for departments like PWD, Irrigation, and Rural Works.

Lecturer (Government Inter College): Recruitment for Group C teaching positions in secondary education. (2026 Mains: Jan 25).

Forest Range Officer (FRO) & Assistant Conservator of Forest (ACF): Specialized exams for the Forest Department.

2. Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UKSSSC)
The UKSSSC handles the majority of Group C (Non-Gazetted) and frontline technical recruitments.

Graduate Level Exams: Common exams for Village Development Officer (VDO), Village Panchayat Development Officer (VPDO), and Assistant Social Welfare Officer.

Intermediate (10+2) Level Exams: For Junior Assistants, Data Entry Operators (DEO), and Tax Collectors.

Uniformed Services: Recruitment for Uttarakhand Police Constable, Sub-Inspector (SI), and Forest Guard.

Assistant Teacher (LT): Recruitment for Licentiate Teacher grades in state schools.

Stenographer & Personal Assistant: Skill-based exams focused on typing and shorthand.

Technical Posts: Includes Junior Engineer (JE), Lab Assistant, and Livestock Extension Officer.

3. Uttarakhand Medical Service Selection Board (UKMSSB)
The UKMSSB is dedicated to staffing the state’s healthcare and medical education sectors.

Nursing Officer: Large-scale recruitment for government hospitals and medical colleges.

Medical Officer (MBBS/Specialist): Direct recruitment for doctors in state health services.

Pharmacist (Allopathic/Ayurvedic): Recruitment for state insurance and medical departments.

Technician Roles: Includes X-Ray Technicians, Lab Technicians, and Dental Hygienists.

Assistant Professor: For faculty positions in Government Medical Colleges.

Other Notable Recruitment Entities
Uttarakhand Board of Technical Education (UBTER): Primarily conducts the JEEP polytechnic entrance, but occasionally handles direct recruitment for Group D staff and Staff Nurses.

State Corporations: Organizations like UPCL (Power), UJVNL (Hydro), and PTCUL (Transmission) often hold independent exams for engineers and accounts officers.
